Questions.

What is Dailies?

Dailies is a daily motivation app. It sends you short motivational quotes through the day, chosen around the topics you follow, and puts one on your Lock Screen and Home Screen. When you want more, there is a feed to swipe through, and nothing to keep up with.

Who is Dailies for?

Anyone who wants a small, steady push without another habit to manage: a line on the Lock Screen between meetings, a calm one at night, a funny one when the day drags.

Do I need to open the app to get my daily motivation?

No. Quotes arrive as notifications, and a fresh one waits on your Lock Screen and Home Screen widget. Open the app when you want to read on, look back at the ones you saved, or write your own.

Can I choose which motivational quotes I get?

Yes. You follow the topics you care about (calm, confidence, humor, work, health, faith, creativity and more), and Dailies picks from those. Change them whenever you like, along with how often you hear from us and the hours you are happy to be reached.

Can Dailies send quotes at random times of the day?

Yes. Pick how many quotes a day you want, and the hours and days you are happy to hear from us, and Dailies spreads them across that window, at different times each day. A quote you did not expect is one you actually read. More in how to get motivational quotes at random times of day.

Can I get Bible verses, philosophy or funny quotes on my widget?

Yes. Follow the Faith topics for Bible verses, the Meaning and Calm topics for lines from Marcus Aurelius, Lao Tzu and Montaigne, or the Humor topics for dry, kind lines. Your widget and your notifications draw from what you follow.

Does Dailies work on the iPhone Lock Screen?

Yes. Dailies has a Lock Screen widget and a Home Screen widget, so a quote is there the moment you pick up your phone, without unlocking or opening anything. Step by step: how to put quotes on your iPhone Lock Screen, and how to add a widget to your Home Screen.

What makes Dailies different from other quote apps?

Quotes come at different moments inside the hours you choose, rather than once at the same time each day, so they still catch your eye. You follow narrow topics, not broad categories, and every quote carries its author's name. And you can make the widget your own, with photographs, typefaces and quiet effects behind the words. There is nothing to keep up with: read one when it arrives, and that's it.

What is a good alternative to the Motivation app?

If you like Motivation but want narrower topics, more ways to style your widget, or quotes that arrive at moments Dailies picks inside your hours rather than on a schedule, try Dailies. The full side by side: Dailies vs Motivation.

Can I save quotes, or write my own?

Both. Heart the ones that land and they are kept under Liked quotes, and you can write your own lines under My quotes. With Premium, Dailies serves both back to you alongside the rest. Your own writing and your hearted quotes are backed up to your iCloud account, so changing or wiping your phone does not lose them.

Is Dailies free?

Yes, there is a free version with a set of topics, backgrounds and typefaces to choose from. Dailies Premium is an optional annual subscription that opens the whole library: every topic, your Liked quotes and My quotes as feeds of their own, every background, every typeface and effect, your own photos and colors behind your quotes, and no wordmark on the pictures you share.

Which devices does Dailies work on?

iPhone and iPad, in portrait or landscape. There is no Android version. Scan the code on this page, or search for Dailies on the App Store.
