Questions

Is it free?

Yes, for you and for them.

Do they need the app?

No. The envelope opens in any browser, on a phone or a computer.

Do you keep the names?

No. The names and the line are inside the link, in the part a browser never sends to a server. We never see them, and nothing is stored.

Can I send it by text or WhatsApp?

Yes. After you seal it, send it as a message, on WhatsApp or by email, or with your phone’s share button.

Can I write my own message?

Yes. Choose Write your own and type it. It goes inside the envelope exactly as you wrote it.
