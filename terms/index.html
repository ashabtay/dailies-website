2. What Dailies is

Dailies is an app for iPhone and iPad that sends you short quotes through the day — a boost, a laugh, a bit of perspective — as notifications, on a Home Screen widget, and in the app itself. You tell it what you care about and how often you want to hear from it, and it picks from a library that ships inside the app.

That is the whole of it. Dailies is not a social network, a messaging service, a journal, a coaching program or a health product.
