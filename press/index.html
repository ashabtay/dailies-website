Describing Dailies

Take whichever length fits, and edit it as you like.

One line

Dailies sends motivational quotes to your Lock Screen and Home Screen, so the right words find you during the day.

A paragraph

Dailies is a motivational quotes app built to be quiet. You pick the topics you care about, such as calm, confidence, humor, work or faith, and the hours you are happy to be reached. Dailies sends quotes as notifications at varied moments inside those hours and keeps a fresh one on the Lock Screen and in the Home Screen widget, so you get a little boost every time you pick up your phone, without opening anything. It is free to download, with an optional Premium subscription.

Who makes it

Amit Shabtay has spent his career building consumer apps. He was part of the team behind Facetune, and built Enlight, a photo editor that won an Apple Design Award in 2017, and Photoleap. He started Dailies in the summer of 2026 to bring the quote he used to add to his monthly product report to more people, and builds it bootstrapped, at home. Amit on LinkedIn.

Itamar Shabtay, his son, is a high school student who runs the Dailies brand and its social channels.

Dailies is run by Amit as a sole trader, with no company behind it and no outside funding. The About page has more on how the quotes are chosen and what the app will not do.

The moving widget

The quote on the Home Screen widget is not a still picture: the background moves slowly behind the words, so the screen feels alive without being loud. It is the part people ask about most, and a still screenshot cannot show it.

Asides: the app notices you

Now and then, instead of a quote, Dailies shows an Aside: a short line about your moment right now. It knows when your battery is almost empty, when it is Monday before 9am, or when you come back after three weeks away. All of it is worked out on the phone itself. Asides need no permissions, no account and no server, and nothing about your moment leaves the phone.
