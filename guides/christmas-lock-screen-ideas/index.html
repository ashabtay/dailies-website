Christmas Lock Screen ideas for iPhone

December is the one month people change their Lock Screen just for the season. Here are five Christmas setups worth copying, with the steps for each, and how to keep your everyday Lock Screen so January is one swipe away.

By Amit Shabtay, who makes Dailies Written on 12 November 2026, for iOS 27

On this page

First, keep your everyday Lock Screen

You don't have to give up the Lock Screen you have. iOS keeps several and lets you swap between them in a second, so make the Christmas one as a new Lock Screen:

  1. Unlock your iPhone and touch and hold the Lock Screen. Stay on the Lock Screen rather than swiping up. Face ID or your passcode has to unlock it first, or touching and holding does nothing.
  2. Tap the + button. This starts a new Lock Screen. The one you have now stays in the list.
  3. Build your Christmas Lock Screen. Pick a wallpaper and add widgets, using one of the setups below.
  4. Tap Add. Then choose whether the Home Screen gets the same picture or its own.

In January, touch and hold the Lock Screen, swipe back to your usual one and tap it. How to change or delete a Lock Screen covers removing the Christmas one.

Five Christmas setups

1. A snow photo with a quote under the clock

A winter photo with sky at the top, so the time reads cleanly: a snowy street, a window with frost on it, a tree lit up in the dark. If the photo has a clear subject, iOS can let it step in front of the clock; the layering button in the editor turns that on and off.

Then tap the space under the time and add a quote widget. Dailies puts a short line from the topics you follow there and changes it about once an hour, so there's something new to read each time you pick up your iPhone. How to put quotes on your iPhone Lock Screen has the steps.

2. Christmas past, on shuffle

Last year's Christmas photos, one at a time. In the gallery, choose Photo Shuffle and pick the photos yourself rather than letting iOS choose. Then set how often it moves on: on tap, on wake, hourly or daily. On wake gives you a different Christmas each time you lift your iPhone.

3. A verse for the season

For a Christmas that's about more than the shopping, keep a Bible verse under the clock all December. In Dailies, follow Scripture for today under Personalize, Topics, and add the Dailies widget under the time. How to put a Bible verse on your iPhone Lock Screen walks through it.

4. Red and green, the simple way

No photo at all. In the gallery, choose Color and pick a deep red or a forest green. Tap the time and set it to a soft cream or white; the date and the widgets take the same color, so the whole screen matches. Add one widget under the time and leave the rest empty. Plain looks best with room around it.

5. Snow falling on your Home Screen

This one belongs on the Home Screen, next to your Christmas Lock Screen. Dailies has an effect called Snow Globe that lets snow fall behind your quote, and it's free for everyone until January 1, 2027.

  1. Choose Snow Globe. In Dailies, open Style and pick Snow Globe under Effects.
  2. Shake it. Shake your iPhone while the app is open and the snow swirls, like a real snow globe.
  3. Add the widget. Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen until the icons jiggle, tap Edit, then Add Widget. Search for Dailies, pick the medium or large size, and tap Add Widget. The Home Screen widget carries the snow too.

Make it switch on by itself

A Lock Screen can be tied to a Focus, so the Christmas one appears whenever that Focus is on: in your Personal Focus at home, say, while your Work Focus keeps the everyday look. How to link a Lock Screen to a Focus shows how.

Questions

Can I have a Christmas Lock Screen without losing my usual one?

Yes. Touch and hold the Lock Screen and tap + to make a new one. Both stay in the list until you delete one, and you can swap between them whenever you like.

How do I put a Christmas picture on my Lock Screen?

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap +, choose Photos and pick the picture. Pinch and drag to place it, then tap Add.

Is Snow Globe in Dailies free?

It's free for everyone until January 1, 2027. After that it's part of Premium again.

How do I change back after Christmas?

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, swipe to your usual one and tap it. You can keep the Christmas one for next year or delete it.
