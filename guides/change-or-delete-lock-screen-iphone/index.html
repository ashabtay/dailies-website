How to change or delete a Lock Screen on iPhone

Most people who search for this want one of three things: a different picture behind the clock, a different Lock Screen they made earlier, or fewer of them. All three start the same way, with a long press on the Lock Screen, and each takes under a minute.

Change your Lock Screen

Everything starts from the same long press:

  1. Unlock the iPhone and stay on the Lock Screen. Let Face ID or your passcode unlock it, but do not swipe up. If the phone is still locked, a long press does nothing.
  2. Touch and hold the Lock Screen. After a moment the screen shrinks into a row of your Lock Screens, with Customize and a + button underneath.
  3. To change this one, tap Customize. Then tap the Lock Screen preview. Swipe to try other filters on the photo, or tap the photo button at the bottom left to pick a different picture.
  4. To switch, swipe and tap. Swipe left or right to another Lock Screen you already have and tap it. It becomes your Lock Screen straight away.
  5. To start fresh, tap +. The wallpaper gallery opens. Pick a photo, Photo Shuffle, or one of Apple's designs.
  6. Tap Done, or Add for a new one. Done saves your change. A new Lock Screen is saved with Add, and then you choose whether the Home Screen gets the same picture or its own.

You can also get there from the Settings app: Settings, Wallpaper, then Customize under the Lock Screen or Add New Wallpaper. It opens the same editor, which helps when a long press on the screen is not working.

Delete a Lock Screen

  1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen until the row of Lock Screens appears.
  2. Swipe to the one you want gone.
  3. Swipe up on it. A trash button appears underneath.
  4. Tap the trash button, then tap Delete This Wallpaper.

Its matching Home Screen goes with it.

A Lock Screen linked to a Focus is deleted the same way. The Focus stays; it just goes back to using whatever Lock Screen is showing when it turns on.

When touching and holding does nothing

  • The phone is not unlocked. The lock icon at the top has to be open. Look at the phone, or enter your passcode, then try again without swiping up.
  • You are pressing too lightly or too briefly. Hold for a full second on an empty part of the screen, away from notifications and widgets.
  • The phone is managed. A work or school iPhone can carry a profile that stops wallpaper changes. Only whoever manages the phone can lift that.
  • A Focus is managing it. If a Focus is on and has its own Lock Screen, you will be editing that one. Turn the Focus off in Control Center to get back to your usual Lock Screen.

What to change it to

If you are changing it because the old one has gone invisible, it is worth putting something on it that changes by itself. Photo Shuffle moves through a set of your photos. A widget in the row under the time can show the weather, your next event or a line worth reading. iPhone Lock Screen ideas for iOS 27 has setups worth copying, and how to customize your iPhone Lock Screen covers everything the editor can do.

The Dailies widget is one of those that changes by itself: a short quote from the topics you follow under the clock, a new one about once an hour, drawn in your clock's color so it fits any wallpaper you pick.

Questions

How do I change my Lock Screen on iPhone?

Unlock the iPhone, stay on the Lock Screen and touch and hold it. Tap Customize to change the one you have, swipe to another and tap it to switch, or tap the plus button to make a new one.

How do I delete a Lock Screen on iPhone?

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, swipe to the one you want to remove, swipe up on it, tap the trash button and tap Delete This Wallpaper. Its matching Home Screen is deleted with it.

Why can't I change my Lock Screen?

The iPhone has to be unlocked first, so look at it or enter your passcode without swiping up, then touch and hold. If it still will not open, the phone may be managed by a work or school profile that stops wallpaper changes.

Can I change only the Lock Screen picture and keep my widgets?

Yes. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap the Lock Screen preview, then tap the photo button at the bottom left and pick a new picture. The clock and widgets stay as they were.

How do I switch between Lock Screens?

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, swipe left or right to the one you want and tap it. To have one appear by itself at certain times, link it to a Focus.
