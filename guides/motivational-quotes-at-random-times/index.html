How to get motivational quotes at random times of day on iPhone

A reminder at the same minute every morning is easy to read for a week, and then it becomes part of the morning, like the kettle. A quote that turns up at a moment you did not plan for gets read. Here is how to have motivational quotes reach your iPhone at random times, inside the hours and on the days you choose.

By Amit Shabtay, who makes Dailies Steps written on 7 November 2026

Why random times work better

Anything that repeats on a schedule you can predict fades into the background. Psychologists call it habituation: the mind stops paying attention to a signal it already knows is coming. A line that arrives at an unexpected minute has no slot in the day to disappear into, so it gets a proper look.

The other half is choosing the hours. A surprise is welcome at eleven in the morning and less so at six on a Sunday. So the setup below has three parts: how many quotes a day, the hours they may reach you, and the days of the week. Inside those, the moments are left to chance.

Set it up in Dailies

  1. Install Dailies and answer the setup questions. Your answers pick the topics your quotes come from. You can change them whenever you like under Personalize, Topics.
  2. Open Personalize, then Daily dose. Personalize is in the bar at the bottom of the feed.
  3. Choose how many you want a day. Use the minus and plus. One a day is a gentle start; more spreads them through the day.
  4. Set the hours to reach you. Under Hours to reach you, set From and Until. Nothing arrives outside them, so a window that closes in the early evening keeps your nights to yourself.
  5. Pick the days. Under Days to reach you, every day starts switched on. Tap a day’s circle to give it the day off: tap Saturday and Sunday and the line above reads “Quotes arrive on weekdays only.”
  6. Go back. What is on the screen when you leave is what is kept, and the new schedule starts straight away.

Dailies asks to send notifications during setup. If you said no then, turn them on in the iPhone's Settings, under Notifications, Dailies; how to turn on notifications for an app has the steps.

How the moments are picked

The screen puts it in one line: quotes arrive at unannounced moments inside this window. Behind that, Dailies divides your window into equal stretches, one for each quote, and picks a random minute inside each stretch. So the quotes are spread across the day rather than bunched together, and no two days fall at quite the same times.

Each one is a short quote from the topics you follow, sized to read in a glance on the Lock Screen. And the whole schedule lives on the iPhone itself, so quotes keep arriving with no signal: on a plane, in a basement, with Wi-Fi off.

Between the quotes

A notification is a moment. For the rest of the day, add the Dailies widget under the clock on your Lock Screen: a new quote is there each time you pick up your iPhone, changing about once an hour. How to put quotes on your iPhone Lock Screen walks through it, and the quotes come from the same topics as your notifications.

If you want fixed times instead

Some people want the opposite: one quote at seven, with the first coffee, every day. If that is you, an app with fixed reminder times suits you better. Motivation, the biggest quote app on iPhone, lets you set reminders through the day, and Dailies vs Motivation compares the two side by side.

The iPhone's own Shortcuts app can run an automation at a time of day too. It runs at the time you set, the same time every day, so it suits a fixed routine rather than a surprise.

Questions

Is there an app that sends motivational quotes at random times?

Yes. Dailies sends motivational quotes at unannounced moments inside the hours you choose. You set how many a day, the window and the days of the week, and it picks the minutes.

How many motivational quotes a day can I get?

As many or as few as you like, starting from one a day. Change it whenever you like under Personalize, Daily dose.

Can I stop quotes arriving at night?

Yes. Set Until to the hour you want them to stop. Nothing arrives outside the window you set.

Can I get quotes on weekdays only?

Yes. Under Days to reach you, tap Saturday and Sunday to give them the day off, and quotes arrive on weekdays only. Any other days work the same way.

Do I need Premium for quotes at random times?

No. How many a day, the window and the days are all on the free plan.
