How to get a funny quote on your iPhone every day

Not every day calls for a pep talk. Some days the best lift is a line that makes you smile at yourself, or at the small absurd things everybody does. Dailies has a whole Humor section, and you can make it a part of what you read or all of it. Here is how, with a few lines to show what it is like.

What the funny quotes are like

They are wry lines from writers, most of them centuries old and still exactly right: dry, kind, and aimed at the reader as often as at anybody else. The Humor section is split into topics, and you follow the ones that sound like you.

Laugh at yourself

Ever since the tortoise's famous victory over the hare, it has thought of itself as a sprinter.

Marie von Ebner-Eschenbach, Aphorisms

I swatted a thousand flies in the evening, and still one woke me at dawn.

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe, Poems

Modern life, honestly

Everyone knows that "just a quarter of an hour" lasts longer than a quarter of an hour.

Georg Christoph Lichtenberg, The Waste Books

Dry observations

Some people critique the Himalayas pebble by pebble.

Victor Hugo, William Shakespeare

Small social disasters

There is no greater torment than being unable to say a name that is right on the tip of your tongue.

Ricardo Palma, Peruvian Traditions

Getting older, badly

Courtesy smooths out wrinkles.

Joseph Joubert, Thoughts, Essays and Maxims

Set it up in Dailies

  1. Open Personalize, then Topics. In Dailies, Personalize is in the bar at the bottom of the feed.
  2. Find Humor. Scroll to the Humor section: Laugh at yourself, Modern life, honestly, Dry observations, Small social disasters, and Getting older, badly.
  3. Follow the ones you want. Tap a topic to follow it. At least one Humor topic is open on the free plan; Premium opens the rest.
  4. Choose how funny the mix is. Your feed is made of what you follow. Keep your other topics for a funny line now and then, or follow only Humor topics for funny lines and nothing else.

The change is immediate: the next quote in your feed, on your widget and in your notifications can come from the topics you just followed.

Where they show up

  • Under the clock. Add the Dailies widget to your Lock Screen and a new line is there every time you pick up your iPhone. Short ones fit best, and most of these are short. How to put quotes on your iPhone Lock Screen has the steps.
  • As a notification. Set how many a day and the hours they may reach you under Personalize, Daily dose, and they turn up at unannounced moments in between. A funny line at a moment you were not expecting is the best kind. Quotes at random times of day explains the setup.
  • On the Home Screen. The Home Screen widget shows the same lines over a photograph of your choosing; how to make an aesthetic quote widget covers the look.

If you want jokes

These lines make you smile rather than laugh out loud, and they lean toward wit rather than punchlines. If what you want is puns, one-liners or memes, a joke app is the better pick. If you want a line with a little truth folded into the joke, this is the place.

Questions

Is there an app that sends a funny quote every day?

Yes. Follow the Humor topics in Dailies and the quotes in your feed, on your widgets and in your notifications come from them. You choose how many a day and the hours they may arrive.

Can I have only funny quotes?

Yes. Under Personalize, Topics, follow the Humor topics and unfollow the rest. Your feed and widgets are made of what you follow.

Are the funny quotes free?

At least one Humor topic is open on the free plan. Premium opens the rest.

What kind of humor is it?

Dry and kind: lines from writers such as Goethe, Montaigne, Victor Hugo and Marie von Ebner-Eschenbach about the small absurd things everybody does. Wit rather than jokes or memes.

Can I put a funny quote on my Lock Screen?

Yes. Add the Dailies widget under the clock on your Lock Screen. It shows a quote from the topics you follow, so following the Humor topics puts funny lines there.
