How to get quotes on your Android home screen and lock screen

On Android a quote can sit in three places: a widget on the home screen, a notification on the lock screen, or a picture set as your wallpaper. They behave nothing alike, and only one of them changes on its own. Here is what each one does, and the steps for all three.

No lock screen widget

Dailies has no lock screen widget on Android. Google has started letting some newer Pixel phones put widgets on the lock screen, but Dailies does not offer one there, so this guide does not count on it. On Android, a quote reaches the lock screen as a notification or as your wallpaper.

The rest of this page uses Dailies for the examples, because that is the app this site is about. The steps for the phone itself come from Google's help pages for Pixel phones and Samsung's for Galaxy phones. Other Android phones keep the same settings in slightly different places, so if a name does not match, search for it in the Settings app.

On an iPhone? The iPhone has a Lock Screen widget, and the steps are different: how to put quotes on your iPhone Lock Screen.

1. A widget on the home screen

This is the one that changes without you doing anything. The Dailies widget shows a quote on your home screen, in the background and typeface you chose in the app, and turns over to a new one on the hour. Its back and forward arrows step between quotes and its heart keeps one, right there on the home screen, without opening the app.

  1. On your home screen, touch and hold an empty space.
  2. Tap Widgets.
  3. Find Dailies in the list and tap it to see its widget.
  4. Touch and hold the widget, slide it to where you want it, and lift your finger.

Or let the app place it: in Dailies, open Personalize → Home screen widget and tap Add to home screen. A few phones do not offer this, so the button only shows where it works.

Once it is on your home screen, touch and hold it and drag its edges to change its size. The longer version, with what to do when Dailies is missing from the list, is in how to add a widget on Android.

2. A quote on the lock screen, as a notification

A widget waits on the home screen for you to look. A notification comes to the lock screen and finds you, without your unlocking anything, and each one brings a new quote.

In Dailies this is set under Personalize → Daily dose: how many you get a day, the hours they are allowed to reach you and the days of the week. Inside that window they come at moments you did not pick, on purpose. A quote at the same minute every morning soon becomes an alarm you swipe away without reading; one that turns up at some unremarkable point in the afternoon still gets read.

For the quote to show on the lock screen, Android has to let it through. Three things decide it:

  1. Notifications on for Dailies. Open Settings → Apps → Dailies → Notifications (the path varies by phone; on a Pixel the same switches are also under Settings → Notifications → App notifications). Check notifications are allowed, with Daily quotes on.
  2. Notifications allowed on the lock screen. On a Pixel, open Settings → Notifications → Notifications on lock screen and keep Show sensitive content on, so the lock screen can show what a notification says and not only that one arrived. On a recent Samsung Galaxy phone, open Settings → Notifications → Hide content while locked and choose Show always. On older Galaxy phones, the lock screen's notification options are under Settings → Lock screen.
  3. Nothing holding it back. Do Not Disturb silences apps it does not let through, and Battery saver can delay delivery.

3. A quote as your wallpaper

The permanent one. You turn a quote into a picture, save it to your gallery and set it as the lock screen wallpaper, so the words are the background rather than something laid on top of it.

  1. In Dailies, tap the share button on a quote, or touch and hold the quote. It goes as a card, in the background you are reading it on.
  2. Choose Save image. The picture lands in your gallery.
  3. On a Pixel, touch and hold an empty space on the home screen, tap Wallpapers & style, then Change wallpaper. On a Samsung Galaxy phone, open Settings → Wallpaper and style, then Change wallpapers.
  4. Pick one of your own pictures: Choose a photo on a Pixel, Gallery on a Galaxy. Find the quote you just saved.
  5. Set it for the Lock screen (or both screens), move and pinch it so the clock does not sit on the words, then tap Apply on a Pixel or Done on a Galaxy.

That last step is the one to take slowly. The clock sits at the top of the lock screen, and a quote in the middle of the picture can end up under it or under your notifications. Move the words, or pinch the picture smaller, before you apply it.

Pictures saved on the free tier carry a small Dailies wordmark at the bottom, in the middle; pictures saved with an active Premium subscription do not. Either way the picture is yours to send to a friend or post. The Terms of Use say what is and is not fair game.

A wallpaper does not change on its own: a quote you set stays until you set another. Save a few you like and swap them by hand when one stops landing.

Using more than one

They stack, and most people who stay with this end up on two of the three. The widget and notifications together is the common pairing: something new whenever you glance at the home screen, plus a few moments in the day when a quote comes to the lock screen on its own. A quote wallpaper sits behind notifications without getting in their way, so the lock screen can carry both.

When it is not working

The widget is stuck on the same quote

Usually the phone saving battery rather than anything in the app. In order:

  • Turn off Battery saver. It can hold back widget updates.
  • Open the Dailies app once.
  • Remove the widget and add it back.

Dailies is not in the widget list

Open the app once after installing it, then look again. If it is still missing, restart your phone.

The notification arrives but the lock screen hides the quote

The lock screen is set to hide what notifications say. On a Pixel, turn on Show sensitive content under Settings → Notifications → Notifications on lock screen. On a recent Galaxy, set Hide content while locked to Show always under Settings → Notifications.

The quotes stopped arriving

Four things, and it is nearly always one of them:

  1. Permission. Settings → Apps → Dailies → Notifications (the path varies by phone), with notifications allowed and Daily quotes on.
  2. Do Not Disturb. Switch it off, or let Dailies through in its settings.
  3. Quotes arrive inside the hours you chose. Open Personalize → Daily dose. If the window runs from 9am to 5pm and you are checking at 8am, nothing is due yet. Inside the window they come at moments you did not pick, on purpose.
  4. Battery saver, which can delay delivery.

Everything else is on the help page, and help@mydailies.app reaches a person if none of it helps.

Questions

Does Dailies have a lock screen widget on Android?

No. On Android, Dailies has a home screen widget only. Some newer Pixel phones have started allowing widgets on the lock screen, but Dailies does not offer one there. A quote still reaches the lock screen as a notification, or as a picture set as your wallpaper.

Does the home screen widget change on its own?

Yes, on the hour. Its arrows also step to the next quote or back to the last one whenever you like, and its heart keeps one, without opening the app.

Can I choose the exact time a quote arrives?

No, and that is on purpose. Under Personalize → Daily dose you choose how many a day, the hours they may reach you and the days of the week. Inside that window they come at moments you did not pick.

Do I need an account to do any of this?

No. Dailies has no sign-up, no email address and no password; there is no login anywhere in the app.

Does it work with no signal?

Yes. The quote library ships inside the app and notifications are scheduled on your phone, so everything except a purchase works in airplane mode.

I have an iPhone. Is it different there?

Yes. The iPhone also has a Lock Screen widget, so there are three ways there too, with different steps. They are in how to put quotes on your iPhone Lock Screen.
