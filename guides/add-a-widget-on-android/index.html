How to add a widget on Android

On most Android phones it is the same three moves: touch and hold the home screen, open Widgets, drag one into place. The words change a little from one phone maker to the next, and that is what this page sorts out, along with what to do when the widget you want is missing or stuck.

Adding a widget

On an iPhone? The steps are in how to add a widget to your Home Screen.

These are Google's own steps, from its Android help, and they hold on most Android phones. Dailies is the example; the same goes for any app that has a widget.

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen, somewhere with no icon or widget on it.
  2. Tap Widgets.
  3. Find the app that has the widget you want, such as Dailies, and tap it to see its widgets.
  4. Touch and hold the widget. Images of your home screens appear.
  5. Slide it where you want it and lift your finger.

Google notes that some of these steps only work on Android 14 and up. On an older phone the menu can look different, but touch and hold, Widgets, drag is still the shape of it.

Two shortcuts many phones have. The widget list often has a search field at the top, which beats scrolling a long list. And touching and holding an app's own icon opens a small menu that, on many phones, leads straight to that app's widgets.

On a Samsung Galaxy

Galaxy phones run One UI, Samsung's own version of Android, and Samsung's help has you pick the widget and tap a button rather than drag it:

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on the home screen.
  2. Tap Widgets at the bottom of the screen. Suggested widgets come first; scroll down for the rest.
  3. Tap the app, then the widget you want. Some widgets come in more than one style or size, and you pick one here.
  4. Tap Add. The widget goes onto the home screen.

Touch and hold a placed widget for Samsung's menu. Settings there changes things like the background color and transparency, where the widget allows it, and Remove takes it off the home screen. Samsung also lets you stack widgets in one spot and swipe between them.

On a Google Pixel

Pixel phones are Google's own, so the steps at the top of this page are the Pixel steps word for word: touch and hold an empty spot, tap Widgets, tap the app, then touch and hold the widget and drag it into place.

  • The list is grouped by app. Each app appears once; tap it to open its widgets. The search field at the top takes the app's name.
  • Some versions let you skip the drag. Tap a widget in the list and a button to add it appears; tap that and the Pixel finds it a place.

Resizing, moving and removing

Resize it

  • Touch and hold the widget, then lift your finger.
  • If the widget can be resized, an outline with dots appears around it.
  • Drag the dots to make it bigger or smaller.
  • Tap outside the widget when you are done.

On a Samsung the outline works the same way: touch and hold a dot and drag it. Only the sizes the app allows are open to you, so a widget may stop growing before the screen runs out.

Move it

Touch and hold the widget and drag it. Images of your home screens appear; slide it to the new spot, or to the edge of the screen to reach another page, and lift your finger.

Remove it

Touch and hold the widget and drag it up to Remove, or, on a Samsung, touch and hold it and tap Remove. That takes the widget off the home screen and nothing else: the app stays installed, with everything in it. Uninstall, which some phones show in the same place, is the one that deletes the app, so check which word you drop it on.

When an app offers to add it for you

Since Android 8, an app can ask your phone to put its widget on the home screen. You tap a button inside the app, often called something like Add to home screen, and Android answers with its own window showing a preview of the widget.

  • Nothing is added until you say so. Confirm in that window and the widget lands on your home screen. Close it and nothing changes.
  • On some phones you can place it yourself by touching and holding the preview and dragging it where you want it.
  • Not every home screen supports this. Apps can check first, so on some phones the button simply is not there. The steps at the top of this page always work.

When it goes wrong

The widget is not in the list

In rough order of how often it is the answer:

  1. Open the app once. On some phones an app's widgets only appear after the app has been opened, and it costs nothing to try.
  2. Search instead of scrolling, and look under the app's name. The list is sorted by app, not by what each widget does.
  3. Check the app has a widget. Not every app does.
  4. Reinstall the app. Uninstall it, install it again, open it once, and look again.
  5. Check for a work profile. If your phone has one, work apps are kept apart from personal ones, and the widget list often shows them on a separate tab. An app installed on one side only shows its widgets there.
  6. On a Samsung, check the layout is not locked. If you cannot add or remove anything, or the options are grayed out, Lock Home screen layout may be on. Turn it off in the home screen settings.

The widget is not updating

Android, not the app, decides when a widget may redraw, and it puts it off to save battery.

  • Turn off Battery saver. On a Samsung it is called Power saving. Both hold back what apps do in the background, widgets included.
  • Check the app's own battery setting. In Settings → Apps, open the app and its battery page. If it is kept from running in the background, allow it.
  • On a Samsung, check the sleeping apps. In the battery settings, under Background usage limits, take the app out of Sleeping apps and Deep sleeping apps.
  • Open the app once. Many apps redraw their widget when they are opened.
  • Remove the widget and add it back.

Your phone does not look like this

The home screen is itself an app, called a launcher, and each phone maker ships its own: Pixel Launcher on Google's phones, One UI Home on Samsung's, and others on Motorola, OnePlus, Xiaomi and the rest. They all keep touch and hold and a Widgets menu, but the words around them move. If you installed a launcher yourself, its widget menu is wherever that launcher puts it, usually behind the same touch and hold. When a label here does not match your phone, look for the word widgets.

The Dailies widget on Android

Dailies is a daily motivation app, and on phones running Android 10 or later it puts a quote on your home screen. What to know about it:

  • Home screen only. There is no Lock Screen widget on Android. A quote can still reach your lock screen as a notification, or as a picture you save to your gallery and set as your wallpaper.
  • The arrows and the heart work in place. Step back or forward through quotes, or heart one, right on the home screen.
  • The tile turns over on the hour. A fresh quote every hour, without opening anything.
  • The app can place it for you. Open Personalize → Home screen widget and tap Add to home screen. A few phones do not offer this, so the button only shows where it works.
  • Battery saver can hold it back. If the tile stops changing, turn off Battery saver and open Dailies once; if that does not help, remove the widget and add it back.

For notifications, open Settings → Apps → Dailies → Notifications (the path varies by phone) and check Daily quotes is on. Everything else is on the help page.

Questions

Why is there no Widgets option when I touch and hold?

You are probably holding an app icon or a widget, which opens that item's own menu. Touch and hold a spot with nothing on it. On some phones, pinching the home screen with two fingers opens the same menu.

Does removing a widget uninstall the app?

No. Remove takes the widget off the home screen and leaves the app, and everything in it, where it was. Uninstall is the one that deletes the app.

How do I make a widget bigger or smaller?

Touch and hold it, lift your finger, and drag the dots on its outline. If no dots appear, that widget comes in one size only.

Can I put the Dailies widget on my lock screen on Android?

No. On Android, Dailies has a home screen widget only. A quote can still reach your lock screen as a notification, or as a picture you save to your gallery and set as your wallpaper.

Why did the Dailies widget stop changing?

Usually the phone saving battery. Turn off Battery saver, open Dailies once, and if that does not help, remove the widget and add it back.
