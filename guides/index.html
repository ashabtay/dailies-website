The places a quote can land

Worth two minutes before you start, because most of the disappointment with this comes from picking a route that was never going to do what somebody hoped.

WhereWhat it is
A notificationA quote that arrives and interrupts, at a moment you did not choose, on the schedule you set
The Lock Screen widgetA short line under the clock, drawn by iOS in a single tone, new about once an hour
The Home Screen widgetA bigger box carrying your background and typeface, with arrows on it
A wallpaperA quote saved as a picture and set as the background, there until you change it

Most people who stick with this end up running two: the widget for when they glance at their iPhone, and notifications for when they are not thinking about it at all.
