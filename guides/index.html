Getting quotes onto your iPhone

A quote can reach you in three places on an iPhone, and they behave nothing alike — one waits for you to look, one comes and finds you, and one never changes at all. These guides walk through each, with the fixes for when the phone decides not to play along.

The three places a quote can land

Worth two minutes before you start, because most of the disappointment with this comes from picking a route that was never going to do what somebody hoped.

WhereWhat it isChanges on its own?
A notificationA quote that arrives and interrupts, at a moment you did not chooseYes, on the schedule you set
The Lock Screen widgetA short line under the clock, drawn by iOS in a single toneYes, about once an hour
The Home Screen widgetA bigger box carrying your background and typeface, with arrows on itYes, about once an hour
A wallpaperA quote saved as a picture and set as the backgroundNo — it is a still, until you change it

Most people who stick with this end up running two: the widget for when they glance at the phone, and notifications for when they are not thinking about it at all. The Lock Screen guide sets all of them up.
