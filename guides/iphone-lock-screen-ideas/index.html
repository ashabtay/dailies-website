iPhone Lock Screen ideas for iOS 27

iOS 27 gave the Lock Screen its biggest set of changes in a few years: a clock that can shrink to the size of the date, wallpapers that extend a photo or come from a sentence you type, and a slider for how see-through the glass is. Here is what is new, and eight setups worth copying.

By Amit Shabtay, who makes Dailies Written on 3 November 2026, for iOS 27

What is new in iOS 27

  • A small clock. The time no longer has to be the biggest thing on the screen. A layout option at the top right of the Font & Color panel shrinks it to sit beside the date at the top, which leaves the whole photo clear.
  • Extend. A photo that is too small or the wrong shape for the screen can have its edges filled in by Apple Intelligence, so it covers the Lock Screen without zooming in on a face. iOS may suggest it while you set the wallpaper. It needs an iPhone that runs Apple Intelligence.
  • Wallpapers from Image Playground. Describe a picture in a sentence, and Image Playground makes it and sets it as your Lock Screen.
  • A Liquid Glass slider. In Settings, Appearance, Liquid Glass, a slider runs from clear to tinted. Tinted makes notifications and controls easier to read over a busy photo.

Eight setups worth copying

1. The clear view

Your best landscape photo, the small clock, and nothing else. It only works with a photo good enough to look at on its own, which is exactly the point of shrinking the clock.

2. One good line

A quiet photo, the clock at full size, and a single wide widget underneath with something worth reading. A quote widget such as Dailies puts a short line from the topics you follow there and changes it about once an hour, so the Lock Screen has something new on it whenever you pick the phone up. The steps are in how to put quotes on your iPhone Lock Screen.

3. Your people, shuffled

Photo Shuffle with the people in your life, changing every time you wake the phone. Turn on the depth effect so faces step in front of the clock.

4. The workday

A plain color or a soft gradient, your next calendar event above the time, and reminders and the weather in the row underneath. Link it to your Work Focus so it appears on its own when the day starts. How to link a Lock Screen to a Focus shows how.

5. Wind down

A dark photo, a dim red or amber clock, and no widgets at all. Link it to your Sleep Focus. A Lock Screen with nothing on it asks nothing of you at eleven at night.

6. Something only you have

An old photo that never fitted the screen, brought back with Extend. Scans of film prints and pictures taken sideways are the ones that gain the most.

7. Made from a sentence

Open the Image Playground option in the wallpaper gallery and describe what you want: a calm sea at dawn in watercolor, your street in the snow. Keep the bottom half plain if you add widgets.

8. The world, live

Apple's Weather or Astronomy wallpaper, which changes with the sky outside or shows the Earth as it is right now. Pair it with the small clock so the view is not covered.

The steps

Every setup above starts the same way:

  1. Unlock the iPhone and touch and hold the Lock Screen. Stay on the Lock Screen rather than swiping up. The row of your Lock Screens appears.
  2. Tap + and pick a wallpaper. Choose a photo, Photo Shuffle, one of Apple's designs, or make one in Image Playground.
  3. Fit the photo. Pinch and drag to place it. If it is too small for the screen, tap Extend to have the edges filled in.
  4. Choose the clock. Tap the time to open Font & Color. Pick a font and color, and use the layout option at the top right for the small clock.
  5. Add widgets. Tap the widget area and pick from the list. A wide widget takes half the row.
  6. Tap Add. Then choose whether the Home Screen gets the same picture or its own.

To change one you already have instead, tap Customize rather than the plus. Everything else the editor can do is in how to customize your iPhone Lock Screen.

Keeping it readable

The test of a Lock Screen is not how it looks on its own but how it looks with three notifications on top. Before you settle on one, check it with a few waiting. If they are hard to read, move the Liquid Glass slider toward tinted, pick a calmer photo, or choose a softer clock color, which the widgets take on too.

Questions

What is new on the Lock Screen in iOS 27?

A small clock that sits beside the date, Extend for filling in the edges of a photo, wallpapers made in Image Playground, and a Liquid Glass slider in Settings, Appearance that runs from clear to tinted.

How do I make the clock smaller on iOS 27?

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap the time to open Font and Color, then choose the small layout with the option at the top right of the panel.

How do I use Extend on my wallpaper?

When you set a photo as your Lock Screen, tap Extend to have Apple Intelligence fill in the edges so the photo covers the screen. It needs an iPhone that runs Apple Intelligence.

How do I make my Lock Screen easier to read?

Move the Liquid Glass slider in Settings, Appearance toward tinted, pick a calmer photo, or choose a softer clock color. Check it with a few notifications showing.

What should I put on my Lock Screen?

Something that changes by itself, so you keep noticing it: Photo Shuffle, a live weather or astronomy wallpaper, or a widget with a short quote under the clock.
