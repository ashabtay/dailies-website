The best motivational quote apps for iPhone

Five apps come up whenever somebody asks for a daily dose of motivation on an iPhone, and they are less alike than their listings make them sound: two hand you quotes, two hand you affirmations, and one plays you a speech. Here is what each one does, who it suits, and the figures from each App Store listing, dated.

This page is written by the person who makes Dailies, one of the five, so read it as a maker's comparison rather than a neutral one. Every figure about another app is from its own App Store listing and dated, and each of them is the better choice for somebody. Where that is, it says so.

The short answer

  • Motivation if you want the biggest, most established quote app, on an Apple Watch as well as your iPhone, with streaks.
  • I am if you want affirmations, sentences about yourself to repeat, rather than quotes.
  • Dailies if you want real control over which quotes you get and how they look, with no ads and no sign-in.
  • ThinkUp if you will actually sit down each day to listen to affirmations recorded in your own voice.
  • Motivate if what moves you is a speaker: video and audio you watch for a few minutes.

Motivation

The biggest quote app on iOS, made by Monkey Taps, with over a million ratings. It sends a handful of quotes through the day, keeps the current one on Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and lets you browse thousands more by category. It is on more devices than anything else here, Apple Watch and Apple TV included, and it counts streaks if that is what keeps you coming back. Its free tier carries ads; premium removes them and unlocks more themes and your own photos.

Best for: the safe, well-reviewed choice, especially on an Apple Watch. Dailies vs Motivation goes into the differences.

I am

Also from Monkey Taps, and the biggest affirmation app in the world. Instead of somebody else's sentence, it hands you one about yourself, in the first person, to be repeated: I am enough, I handle hard things. It has widgets for the Home Screen and the Lock Screen and runs on a Mac too.

Best for: people for whom affirmation is the practice they want. Some readers find a strong affirmation hard to say on a bad day; Dailies vs I am is about that difference.

Dailies

A quote app built around two choices: which quotes reach you, and what they look like. Rather than broad categories, you follow narrow topics, such as when the worry won’t sit down or you’re allowed to be a beginner, and your feed is made only of those. Each quote sits on a photograph you choose, in a typeface you choose, with optional quiet motion effects. You set how many quotes a day and the hours they may arrive in, and the app picks the moments. There are no ads at any tier and no account.

Best for: people who want a quote app that feels like theirs, in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German or Simplified Chinese. The trade: it is new, and it runs on iPhone and iPad only.

ThinkUp

An affirmation app with a twist: you record the affirmations in your own voice, over music, and listen back. It is a session rather than a glance, minutes with headphones on, and it logs mindful minutes to Apple Health.

Best for: people who will make time to listen every day. Dailies vs ThinkUp compares the effort each one asks of you.

Motivate

Video and audio rather than text: speeches and motivational talks sorted into playlists, with new ones added daily. Nothing on this list reproduces what a good speech can do before a workout, and nothing on it asks more of your time.

Best for: people who are moved by a voice and have a few minutes and headphones. Dailies vs Motivate is about watching versus glancing.

How to choose

Ask what you will actually be doing when the motivation reaches you. If the answer is queueing for coffee with one hand free, you want a line you can read in three seconds: Motivation, I am or Dailies. If it is sitting on a train with headphones, ThinkUp or Motivate can give you more. Then ask whether you want somebody else's words or a sentence about yourself, which splits the first three.

All five are free to try. Installing two for a fortnight and keeping the one you still read is a perfectly good way to decide.

Questions

What is the best motivational quotes app for iPhone?

Motivation is the biggest and best reviewed, with over a million ratings. Dailies is the one to pick if you want to choose narrow topics and control how each quote looks, with no ads. I am is the leader if you want affirmations rather than quotes.

Which quote apps have a Lock Screen widget?

Motivation, I am and Dailies all have Lock Screen widgets that show the current line under the clock. Motivate has a Home Screen widget only, and ThinkUp lists none.

Is there a motivational quotes app without ads?

Dailies has no ads at any tier, free or paid. Motivation shows ads in its free tier and removes them with premium.

What is the difference between a quote app and an affirmation app?

A quote app gives you a line somebody else wrote, to read. An affirmation app gives you a sentence about yourself, in the first person, to repeat. Motivation and Dailies are quote apps; I am and ThinkUp are affirmation apps.

Which motivation apps work on Apple Watch?

Motivation, I am and ThinkUp have Apple Watch apps. Dailies and Motivate run on the iPhone, and Dailies on iPad too.
