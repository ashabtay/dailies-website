Dailies vs Daily Quote

Daily Quote is one of the oldest quote apps on the App Store, and its idea is in the name: one well chosen quote a day, on a photograph, with a reminder to go and read it. Dailies starts from the same place and then hands you the controls: which topics, how many quotes, which hours, and how the words look.

The short answer

Choose Daily Quote if you want one good quote every morning and nothing to configure. It has been doing that since 2012, it is rated 4.8 across 66,000 ratings, it runs on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, and it is free to use with ads. Its paid extra is unusual: Siri reads quotes aloud from the Lock Screen when you ask.

Choose Dailies if one general quote is too broad a net. You pick narrow topics, how many quotes arrive, the hours and the days, and each one lands as a notification and on a widget, on a background and in a typeface you chose.

The latest version on Daily Quote’s listing, 17.5.0, is dated 31 May 2024.

One quote for everyone, or your own topics

A quote of the day is a lovely format and a blunt one. The same line goes to the person starting a new job and the person sitting in a hospital parking lot, so it has to be general enough to mean something to both. Daily Quote’s collection is hand-picked and its listing is full of people who have kept it for years, which says the format works for a lot of mornings.

Dailies aims smaller. Instead of one broad stream you follow topics, and they are specific: when the worry won’t sit down, you’re allowed to be a beginner, say it while they’re here. Your feed is built only from what you follow, and you can change it on a bad week and change it back after. Heart the lines that land and they are kept under Liked quotes; write your own under My quotes, and with Premium both join the rotation.

When it reaches you

Daily Quote’s listing describes a reminder to view your daily quote, so the quote waits inside the app and the notification invites you in.

In Dailies the notification is the quote. You set how many you want, the hours you are happy to hear from it and the days, and it picks the moments inside that window without announcing them. A Lock Screen widget and a Home Screen widget carry the same line between notifications, so a glance at your iPhone is enough even if you never open the app.

How it looks

Daily Quote pairs its quotes with hand-picked photography and lets you set a background photo of your own. Making quote wallpapers, with control of font size, color and layout, happens in a separate free app from the same maker, QWP.

In Dailies the styling is part of the app: photographic backgrounds, typefaces and motion effects, plus your own photos and colors behind the words with Premium. Whatever you choose is what shows on the widgets and in the pictures you share.

What it costs

Daily Quote is free to download and use, with ads. On 3 November 2026 its US listing showed ad-free use at $3.99 a month, $14.99 a year or $34.99 once, and Audio Quotes at $4.99 a month or $35.99 a year, alongside smaller one-off purchases such as extra quote packs at $1.99.

Dailies has a substantial free tier with no ads at any level. Dailies Premium is an optional subscription that opens every topic, the full background, typeface and effect library, your own photos and colors, and your Liked quotes and My quotes as feeds of their own. It is priced in your currency and shown in full before you confirm.

App Store prices in this category change often and are frequently tested. Check the listing before you buy.

Questions

Does Dailies send a quote of the day?

It can send just one, or several: you choose how many quotes a day, the hours and the days, and Dailies picks the moments inside that window. Each one comes from the topics you follow.

Does Dailies have ads?

No, at any tier. Daily Quote’s listing sells ad-free use as a purchase.

Can I add my own quotes?

In both. Dailies keeps them under My quotes, and with Premium they come back around in the feed, in notifications and on the widgets. Daily Quote’s listing also lets you add quotes.

Can either read quotes aloud?

Daily Quote can, through Siri, as a paid feature called Audio Quotes. Dailies is text only.

Can I get quotes on my Lock Screen?

In Dailies, yes: there is a Lock Screen widget as well as a Home Screen one, and scheduled quotes arrive as notifications through the day. Daily Quote’s listing describes quote wallpapers you can set as a Lock Screen background, and Siri reading quotes from the Lock Screen.

Which languages do they come in?

Daily Quote’s listing gives English. Dailies comes in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Simplified Chinese and Japanese.
