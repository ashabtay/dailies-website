Dailies vs Mantra

Mantra is an affirmations app: first-person sentences, mostly beginning with I am, that you read, hold and say until they stick. Hold down the button and the affirmation glows. Dailies is a quotes app: a line somebody else wrote, about the thing you are going through, arriving at a moment you did not plan. Both aim to change how a day feels. They ask very different things of you.

The short answer

Choose Mantra if affirmations work for you and you want a practice around them. It is rated 4.8 across 34,000 ratings, it comes in ten languages, it runs on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, and it goes beyond reading: you can record your own voice and listen on repeat, and follow manifestation and guided affirmation sessions with ambient sound.

Choose Dailies if saying “I am confident” to yourself has never quite landed, and a good line from somebody who has been there does more. Dailies sends quotes from the topics you follow, at times you set, to your Lock Screen.

First person, or someone else’s words

An affirmation asks you to say something about yourself and believe it a little more each time. For many people that repetition is the point, and Mantra builds the whole app around it: hold the button and the sentence lights up, swipe for the next one, record yourself and play it back.

A quote works from the other side. It is not a claim about you, so there is nothing to argue with on a day you do not feel it. It is somebody else, often long ago, describing the thing you are in and how they got through it. Dailies is built on that: thousands of lines from writers and thinkers, sorted into narrow topics like you’re allowed to be a beginner or when the worry won’t sit down, so the one that arrives is about your week.

If you are not sure which suits you, the question is simple: does “I am enough” steady you, or make you want to argue back? Our piece on whether affirmations work goes into what the research says.

When it reaches you

Mantra’s listing describes smart daytime and bedtime reminders as push notifications, and a fresh daily affirmation; you can read as many more as you like by swiping in the app.

In Dailies the notification is the quote. You set how many you want, the hours you are happy to hear from it and the days, and it picks the moments inside that window without telling you when. A Lock Screen widget and a Home Screen widget carry the same line in between, so it reaches you even on a day you never open the app.

How it looks

Mantra lets you personalize its theme with background images, background videos and fonts, and share affirmations in portrait or square format.

Dailies does the same job for quotes: photographic backgrounds, typefaces and motion effects, plus your own photos and colors with Premium, carried through to the widgets and to the pictures you share.

What it costs

On 3 November 2026 Mantra’s US listing showed Premium monthly at $4.99 and $9.99, three months at $9.99, yearly at prices from $14.99 to $59.99, and lifetime at $74.99. That spread usually means different people are shown different prices.

Dailies has a substantial free tier with no ads at any level. Dailies Premium is an optional subscription that opens every topic, the full background, typeface and effect library, your own photos and colors, and your Liked quotes and My quotes as feeds of their own. It is priced in your currency and shown in full before you confirm.

App Store prices in this category change often and are frequently tested. Check the listing before you buy.

Questions

What is the difference between an affirmation and a quote?

An affirmation is a sentence about yourself, usually in the first person, that you repeat until it feels true. A quote is somebody else’s sentence that happens to fit what you are going through. Mantra is built on the first, Dailies on the second.

Does Dailies have affirmations?

Dailies is a quotes app. Some of its lines are encouraging in the way affirmations are, but they come from other people rather than asking you to speak them about yourself.

Can I record my voice in Dailies?

No. Dailies is text only. Mantra lets you record your voice and listen on repeat.

Can I get quotes on my Lock Screen?

In Dailies, yes: there is a Lock Screen widget as well as a Home Screen one, and scheduled quotes arrive there as notifications through the day. Mantra’s listing describes reminders but not a widget.

Which languages do they come in?

Mantra’s listing gives English and nine more, including Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Chinese. Dailies comes in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Simplified Chinese.
