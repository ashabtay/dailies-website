Four shapes

All five are filed under Health & Fitness and all of them promise to make your day better. What separates them is what you have to be doing at the moment they reach you.

AppWhat reaches youWhat you have to do
DailiesA personalized quoteRead it. Three seconds, on the Lock Screen.
MotivationOne quote, plus a catalog to browseRead it, or go looking for more.
I amA sentence written in the first personSay it about yourself, and mean it.
ThinkUpYour own voice, recorded, over musicPut headphones on and listen.
MotivateVideo and audio from speakersSit down and watch for a few minutes.

If you have three seconds and your hands are full, the last two are the wrong shape however good they are. If you want ten proper minutes a day, a quote on a Lock Screen will not be enough. Plenty of people want both at different hours, which is why a lot of phones carry two of these.

Where Dailies sits

Dailies is a quotes app, so its nearest neighbors are Motivation and I am. What it does differently is narrow: you build the feed out of topics, you choose how the words look, and it does not tell you when the next quote is coming.

Rather than one broad category, you follow topics — grouped under headings like Calm, Confidence, Humor, Work, Love and relationships, Faith, Parenting, Gratitude, Keep going and Self-kindness, and specific underneath: when the worry won’t sit down, you’re allowed to be a beginner, make friends as an adult, say it while they’re here. Your feed is made only of the topics you follow, so a quote about something you did not ask for does not arrive at all.

The timing is the other half. You choose how many quotes you want, the hours you are happy to hear from it, and the days. Dailies picks the actual moments inside that window, so the quote lands as a small interruption in an ordinary afternoon rather than as the 9:00 alarm you learn to swipe away.

The library

The whole quote library ships inside the app, and a generous slice of it is free. There is no feed to load and no account to make: every part of Dailies except the App Store purchase itself works with the phone in airplane mode.
