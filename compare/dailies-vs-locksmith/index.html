Dailies vs locksmith

locksmith does one charming thing: you and your best friends link up, and whatever one of you writes appears on the others’ Lock Screens. Dailies also puts words on your Lock Screen, but they are quotes, picked from the topics you follow and changed through the day. Both are small apps with one idea. The difference is who is talking.

The short answer

Choose locksmith if what you want on your Lock Screen is your friends. It is rated 4.8 across 58,000 ratings, it takes three steps to set up (add the widget, link with your friends, write a message), and a note from someone who knows you can do things no quote can.

Choose Dailies if you want something there on the days nobody writes. Dailies keeps its Lock Screen widget full from a large quote library, sorted into topics you follow, and sends quotes as notifications inside the hours you choose.

The latest version on locksmith’s listing, 1.5.7, is dated 4 April 2023.

Who is talking

A note from a friend on your Lock Screen is personal in a way nothing else on the iPhone is. It is an in-joke, a good luck before an exam, a reminder that somebody is thinking of you. That is locksmith’s whole appeal, and it depends on the friends: the widget is as lively as the people writing to it.

Dailies does not depend on anybody. The words come from a quote library, chosen from topics you follow, as specific as you’re allowed to be a beginner or make friends as an adult. You will not know the person who said it, but you will often recognize the feeling. And when a quote is too good to keep to yourself, you can share it as a picture, in the look you gave it.

When the words change

On locksmith the Lock Screen changes when a friend writes something new.

In Dailies it changes on a rhythm you set. You choose how many quotes you want, the hours you are happy to hear from it and the days, and Dailies picks the moments inside that window, sending each quote as a notification and turning the widgets over to it. On a quiet day, it still arrives.

What it costs

locksmith is free to download. On 3 November 2026 its US listing showed one in-app purchase, All Access at $4.99.

Dailies has a substantial free tier with no ads at any level. Dailies Premium is an optional subscription that opens every topic, the full background, typeface and effect library, your own photos and colors, and your Liked quotes and My quotes as feeds of their own. It is priced in your currency and shown in full before you confirm.

App Store prices change. Check the listing before you buy.

Questions

Can Dailies send a quote to a friend’s Lock Screen?

No. Dailies fills your own widgets and notifications. You can share any quote as a picture, in the look you gave it, through the usual share sheet.

Does the Dailies widget change without anyone writing?

Yes. It turns over to new quotes from the topics you follow, and the same quotes arrive as notifications inside the hours you set.

Is locksmith still updated?

The latest version on its US listing on 3 November 2026 was 1.5.7, dated 4 April 2023.

Can I write my own lines in Dailies?

Yes, under My quotes. With Premium they come back around in the feed, in notifications and on the widgets, mixed with quotes from your topics.

Which languages do they come in?

locksmith’s listing gives English. Dailies comes in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Simplified Chinese and Japanese.
