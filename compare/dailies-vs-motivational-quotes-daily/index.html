Dailies vs Motivational Quotes Daily

Motivational Quotes Daily started as a quotes app and has grown into a self-help toolbox: quotes updated every hour, celebrity speeches, inspirational videos, sleep music, guided meditations, breathing exercises, and in its latest versions a habit tracker and a focus timer. Dailies does one of those things, quotes, and puts its effort into which quote reaches you, when, and how it looks.

The short answer

Choose Motivational Quotes Daily if you want one app for the whole wellbeing shelf. It is rated 4.9 across 38,000 ratings, it runs on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, it has quote widgets, and next to the quotes it carries speeches, sleep sounds, meditation, breathing, motivation alarms, a habit tracker and a Pomodoro timer.

Choose Dailies if quotes are what you came for and you want them aimed. You follow narrow topics, choose how many quotes arrive and in which hours, and choose the background, typeface and effect they arrive on.

A toolbox or one tool

There is a real case for the toolbox. If you want to fall asleep to rain, do a breathing exercise at lunch, time a focus session and read a quote, one app that does all of it is one app fewer. Motivational Quotes Daily keeps adding to that list, and its recent release notes are a run of new tools: alarms, a habit tracker, a Pomodoro timer, a personal feed.

The case for one tool is attention. Every feature is something to find, learn and ignore when you do not need it. Dailies keeps the app to the quote and spends the effort there: topics narrow enough to fit a particular week (when the worry won’t sit down, make friends as an adult), and a feed built only from the ones you follow, so a line about something you did not ask for does not arrive at all.

When it reaches you

Motivational Quotes Daily sends daily quote reminders on a schedule you set, and its quote widgets keep a line on the Home Screen.

Dailies goes one step further on timing. You set how many quotes you want, the hours you are happy to hear from it and the days, and it chooses the moments inside that window without announcing them, so the quote lands as a small surprise rather than as the alarm you learn to swipe away. The notification is the quote itself, and a Lock Screen widget and a Home Screen widget carry the same line in between.

How it looks

Motivational Quotes Daily shows image quotes and text quotes, and you can download or share quote images to the usual social apps.

In Dailies, how the words look is yours to set: photographic backgrounds, typefaces and motion effects, plus your own photos and colors with Premium. The same look carries through to the widgets and to the pictures you share.

What it costs

Motivational Quotes Daily’s description calls it always 100% free. On 3 November 2026 the same US listing also showed in-app purchases: Premium Motivation at $9.99 and $29.99, Premium Pro Annual at $19.99 and a lifetime purchase at $69.99. Check what the free version includes on your own iPhone before you count on it.

Dailies has a substantial free tier with no ads at any level. Dailies Premium is an optional subscription that opens every topic, the full background, typeface and effect library, your own photos and colors, and your Liked quotes and My quotes as feeds of their own. It is priced in your currency and shown in full before you confirm.

App Store prices in this category change often and are frequently tested. Check the listing before you buy.

Questions

Is Motivational Quotes Daily free?

Its listing describes it as always 100% free, and on 3 November 2026 the same listing showed in-app purchases from $9.99 to $69.99. Dailies has a free tier with no ads, and an optional Premium subscription.

Does Dailies have meditation or sleep sounds?

No. Dailies is quotes only, and puts its effort into which quote reaches you, when, and how it looks.

Can I get quotes on my Lock Screen?

In Dailies, yes: there is a Lock Screen widget as well as a Home Screen one, and scheduled quotes arrive there as notifications through the day. Motivational Quotes Daily’s listing mentions quote widgets.

Can I write my own quotes?

In both. Dailies keeps yours under My quotes, and with Premium they come back around in the feed, in notifications and on the widgets. Motivational Quotes Daily added its own version in a recent release.

Which languages do they come in?

Motivational Quotes Daily’s listing gives English. Dailies comes in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Simplified Chinese and Japanese.
