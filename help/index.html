Getting started

What happens when I first open Dailies?

A short 3-minute setup that allows Dailies to personalize the quotes to you, and then you are done.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no email address and no password — Dailies has no accounts at all.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad, in portrait or landscape.

Notifications

My quotes have stopped arriving

Almost always one of these three, in this order:

  1. Permission. Go to iOS Settings → Dailies → Notifications and check Allow Notifications is on, with Lock Screen ticked.
  2. A Focus mode. Sleep, Work and Do Not Disturb silence apps that are not on their allow-list. Add Dailies to the Focus you use, or check Settings → Focus.
  3. Your delivery hours. Open Personalize → Daily dose and look at the window you set. If it runs 9am–5pm and you are checking at 8am, nothing is due yet.

One more, easily missed: Low Power Mode makes iOS deprioritise background work, which can delay delivery.

If all of that looks right, open the app once. Opening it prompts Dailies to rebuild its schedule.

How many can I get a day?

Between one and twelve. Set it in Personalize → Daily dose, along with the earliest and latest hour you want to hear from the app. Dailies spreads them evenly through that window rather than bunching them together.

It is too much / not enough

Personalize → Daily dose, and move the number. Turning it down to one a day is a perfectly good way to use Dailies, and the change takes effect immediately.

Can I turn notifications off but keep the app?

Yes. Switch them off in iOS Settings → Dailies → Notifications.

The Home Screen widget

How do I add it?
  1. Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen until the icons jiggle.
  2. Tap + in the top corner.
  3. Search for Dailies and choose it.
  4. Swipe to the size you want, tap Add Widget, then Done.

Dailies offers a medium and a large widget. There is no small size — a quote needs the room to stay readable.

There is an animated walkthrough of exactly this inside the app, under Personalize → Home screen widget.

How often does the widget change?

You choose the cadence in Personalize → Home screen widget: hourly, every four hours, every eight hours, or once a day.

The widget is stuck on one quote

Usually iOS conserving battery. Try, in order:

  • Turn off Low Power Mode — it aggressively throttles widget refreshes.
  • Open the Dailies app once, which pushes a fresh quote to the widget straight away.
  • Remove the widget and add it back.

Making it yours

What can I change?
  • Backgrounds — a large set of photographs, a range of gradients, your own photo, or a solid colour you mix yourself.
  • Typeface — a couple of dozen faces, and you set the text size.
  • Effects — drifting dust, falling snow, mist, fireflies, meteors, lens flare, embers and more.

All of it lives under Theme.

Can I use my own photo?

Yes — pick one from your library and it sits behind your quotes. This is a Dailies Premium feature, as is mixing your own solid colour.

Does the app follow my phone's dark mode?

Yes! The app supports dark mode.

Privacy and your data

What does Dailies know about me?

Your name, your goals and needs, your reminder settings, your favourites and your chosen background.

See the Privacy Policy for more details.

Why did it ask about tracking?

That is Apple's standard App Tracking Transparency prompt. Dailies advertises, and it asks so it can tell which advert led you to download it.

You can change your answer any time in Settings → Apps → Dailies → Allow Tracking, or under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Premium and billing

What do I get with Dailies Premium?

Every background, every typeface and every ambient effect in the app, plus the two things you make yourself: your own photos and your own colour behind your quotes. It also removes the small Dailies wordmark from pictures you export.

What does it cost, and is there a trial?

Premium is an annual subscription, normally offered with a free trial. Price can be found in the app's subscription screen.

How do I stop the trial before it charges me?

Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, in Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Dailies → Cancel Subscription. You keep Premium until the trial's last day.

How do I cancel?

In Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Dailies → Cancel Subscription. Apple handles subscriptions, so it has to be done there rather than in the app.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. That catches a lot of people out, across every app on the store.

You keep Premium until the end of the period you have already paid for. After that the app reverts to the free set — nothing you made is deleted, and a background or face you can no longer use simply falls back to a free one.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are Apple's to give — request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We have no ability to issue them, since the payment never reaches us directly.

If something in the app did not work as promised, please do email us. It helps to know, and we can often fix it.

I paid and the features have not unlocked

Try Restore purchases on the Premium screen first — that is what it is for, and it fixes most cases. Check you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.
