Words of encouragement for a hard day

For the week that will not let up, or the day that went wrong.

  • Look at what you have lived through, and still you hope.

  • Hard things have shown you how capable you are.

  • You have taken every hard turn and kept your heart.

  • You can expect less of yourself today and still be proud.

  • You will not feel like yourself for a while. That is not permanent.

  • You will look back on this week from somewhere you cannot imagine yet.

  • Every hill you have climbed made the next one a little easier.

  • You are allowed to feel hopeful before you know how it ends.

Encouraging words before a big day

For an exam, an interview, a first day or a conversation they have been dreading.

  • Today you get to be bold.

  • Trust yourself to figure it out as you go.

  • You can take a risk with a steady heart.

  • Your friends are not surprised when you do well. They saw it coming.

  • Other people trust your judgment. You can trust it too.

  • Your mind is sharp, and it keeps getting sharper.

  • Let today be the day you believe a little bigger.

  • You have strengths you use so often you forget they are strengths.

Words of encouragement for a fresh start

For a new job, a new city, or starting over after something ended.

  • The next chapter can look nothing like the last one.

  • You can grow roots in a new place.

  • You have faced bigger unknowns and found your feet.

  • You are someone who rebuilds, and rebuilds well.

  • Being new means everything is still ahead of you.

  • What you begin now can grow for years.

  • The day ahead is open. Step into it with hope.

  • There are kinds of happiness you have not tried yet.

Encouraging quotes for a friend, just because

No occasion needed. These say what people rarely say out loud.

  • Your people are lucky to have you in their corner.

  • Your kindness leaves a mark that lasts.

  • There is something good in you that no one can take away.

  • There is a lot to admire in the way you live.

  • You have helped someone feel less alone.

  • The world is better for your way of seeing it.

  • Many, as they head into the forest, will be comforted by your light.

    Amado Nervo

  • What you are doing takes real strength. Notice it.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, for you and for them.

Do they need the app?

No. The envelope opens in any browser, on a phone or a computer.

Do you keep the names?

No. The names and the line are inside the link, in the part a browser never sends to a server. We never see them, and nothing is stored.

Can I send it by text or WhatsApp?

Yes. After you seal it, send it as a message, on WhatsApp or by email, or with your phone’s share button.

Can I write my own message?

Yes. Choose Write your own and type it. It goes inside the envelope exactly as you wrote it.
