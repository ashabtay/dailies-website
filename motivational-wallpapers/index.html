How to set it as your Lock Screen wallpaper

On iPhone

  1. Tap Save this wallpaper, then Save Image. The picture goes to your Photos.
  2. Open Settings → Wallpaper and tap Add New Wallpaper.
  3. Tap Photos and choose the picture.
  4. Tap Add, then Set as Wallpaper Pair, or Customize Home Screen for something calmer behind your apps.

On Android

  1. Tap Save this wallpaper. The picture goes to your Downloads.
  2. Open it in Google Photos or your Gallery app.
  3. Tap the menu (the three dots) and choose Use as or Set as wallpaper.
  4. Choose Lock screen.

Questions

Are these wallpapers free?

Yes. Save as many as you like. Each one carries a small mydailies.app mark near the bottom edge.

What size are they?

1080 by 2340 pixels, the tall shape of a modern phone screen. Your phone scales the picture to fit, and the words stay clear of the clock.

Will the clock cover the quote?

No. The words sit in the lower half of the picture, below the clock and the row where Lock Screen widgets go. If you have stretched your clock extra tall, make it smaller while you customize the Lock Screen.

Who wrote the lines?

Most were written for Dailies. The others are by writers whose work is in the public domain, and their names are under the line.

Can I send one to someone?

Yes. Save it and send the picture, or seal a line in an envelope on words of encouragement.
