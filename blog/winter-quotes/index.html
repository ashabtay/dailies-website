Winter quotes, snow quotes, and how to enjoy the cold months

Winter can be something to get through, or something to enjoy. Research from the far north of Norway suggests the second is more within reach than it sounds. Here's how, with winter and snow quotes for the dark months.

By Amit Shabtay, who makes Dailies Published 13 November 2026

What Norway knows about winter

In Tromsø, in the far north of Norway, the sun doesn't rise above the horizon for around two months each winter. You might expect people there to dread it. Many don't. The psychologist Kari Leibowitz spent a year there studying why, and in 2020 she and Joar Vittersø published what they found in the International Journal of Wellbeing: people who saw winter as something to enjoy, with its own pleasures, reported higher life satisfaction. And the further north people lived, the more positive their view of winter tended to be.

A positive wintertime mindset isn't pretending the cold is warm. It's noticing what winter offers that summer can't.

Four ways to enjoy the cold months

Make it cozy on purpose

Norwegians call it koselig: candles, blankets, a warm drink, good company, an evening with nowhere to be. The dark is the reason to light a candle at four in the afternoon.

Nothing is sweeter than watching the frost outside from your corner by the fire.

George Sand, Handsome Laurence

In winter nature invites us to family life by the fire, and it is out in the open country that the season's rare fine days can be felt and enjoyed.

George Sand, Story of My Life

Go outside anyway

The other Norwegian word is friluftsliv, open-air living, in every season. A walk in the middle of the day, when the light is best, changes how the rest of a winter day feels. Dress for it, and the weather stops being the point.

Winter is beautiful in the country, whatever people say.

George Sand, Story of My Life

Winter has its charms too. We feel freer to spread out when the trees stand so ghostly and see-through before us. They are nothing, but they hide nothing either.

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe, Elective Affinities

Let it be a slower season

Nature doesn't try to grow in January. Winter is a good time to read more, sleep a little longer, and let some plans wait for spring without feeling behind.

Who wants to get to the bottom of everything at once? When the snow melts, we will see. No more effort helps now; if they are roses, they will bloom.

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe, Poems

Keep an eye on the light

After the shortest day in December, the days start to lengthen, a minute or two at a time. Noticing it is one of winter's small pleasures.

God, who keeps us in this hard place, gives each day its joy, even in winter.

Alphonse de Lamartine, Jocelyn

Snow quotes

Fresh snow is full of tiny pockets of air that soak up sound, which is why a street goes so quiet after a snowfall. These lines have some of that quiet in them.

Let our lives be pure as snow-fields, where our footsteps leave a mark, but not a stain.

Madame Swetchine

When I had suffered long, when the hours were traps for me, you appeared as the welcoming light that shines in windows in winter, deep in the evening, on the snow.

Émile Verhaeren, The Sunlit Hours

Let the snow pile up outside, let it hail and storm and rattle at my window; I will never complain, for I carry my love's image and the joy of spring in my heart.

Heinrich Heine, Book of Songs

Winter quotes

Laughter is sunshine: it drives winter from the human face.

Victor Hugo, Les Misérables

Oh, winter, my friend! A thousand times welcome, my somber, stern companion. Are you not the happy herald of mild May and smiling April?

Rosalía de Castro, Beside the River Sar

And though in your winter you deny your spring, Yet spring, reposing within you, smiles in her drowsiness and is not offended.

Kahlil Gibran, The Prophet

For behold, the winter is past. The rain is over and gone. The flowers appear on the earth.

Song of Solomon 2:11-12, Holy Bible

Questions

How can I enjoy winter more?

Treat it as a season with its own pleasures. Make evenings cozy on purpose, get outside in the middle of the day when the light is best, let some plans wait for spring, and notice the days growing longer after December.

What is a positive wintertime mindset?

Seeing winter as something to enjoy rather than endure. Kari Leibowitz and Joar Vittersø found in Norway that people with this view reported higher life satisfaction, and that it was more common the further north people lived.

What is a good quote about snow?

Kahlil Gibran's "And like seeds dreaming beneath the snow your heart dreams of spring" is one of the loveliest. For something short, Madame Swetchine's line about lives pure as snow-fields is a good choice.

Why is it so quiet after it snows?

Fresh snow is full of tiny pockets of air that absorb sound, so a snowy street sounds hushed. The quiet fades as the snow packs down or melts.
