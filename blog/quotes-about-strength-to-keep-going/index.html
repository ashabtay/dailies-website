Quotes about strength to keep going

Strength is rarely the loud kind. Mostly it looks like getting up again, resting when you have to, and taking the next step without knowing how far the road goes. These quotes are for that kind of strength, and the last few are short enough to send to someone who needs them.

By Amit Shabtay, who makes Dailies Published 24 November 2026

For when you're tired but still going

Tired and still going is a kind of strength nobody claps for. It counts anyway.

The end of it is the part people keep. Whatever you're feeling today, it isn't the last thing you'll feel.

Don't let your spirit give up while your body is still going.

Marcus Aurelius, Meditations

I didn't lose heart, in spite of everything.

Santiago Ramón y Cajal, Recollections of My Life

That one is from the memoirs of Santiago Ramón y Cajal, the Spanish scientist who won the Nobel Prize for his work on the brain. Some years, that sentence is the whole story, and it's enough.

For remembering you've done harder things

You've already come through more than you tend to remember.

At first I almost lost heart; I thought I could never bear it. And yet I bore it after all. Just don't ask me how.

Heinrich Heine, Book of Songs

The last line is the honest part. Nobody gets through hard things gracefully. You get through them, and afterward you can't quite explain how.

How many things we never thought we could do, and did anyway! How many blows that did us no harm at all!

Paul Claudel, The Humiliation of the Father

The courage you have already shown is often the best part of the courage you have now.

Madame Swetchine, Thoughts

Weight reveals the palm tree's strength: it rises with the load.

Diego de Saavedra Fajardo, The Idea of a Christian Political Prince

An image from the 1600s: a palm tree that doesn't snap under the weight, but keeps growing upward with it.

For the quiet kind of strength

Strength isn't only pushing. Resting counts. So does waiting until you have more in you.

Poor little bird, if you cannot fly by yourself yet, then stay down with patience until you have more strength.

Angelus Silesius, The Cherubinic Wanderer

A bow cannot stay drawn forever, and frail human nature cannot keep going without some honest recreation.

Miguel de Cervantes, Don Quixote

This is from Don Quixote, a book about someone who almost never stops. Even there, the bow gets to rest.

The human heart is at once the tenderest thing there is and the toughest.

Victor Hugo, Deeds and Words

Tender and tough at once. You don't have to pick one.

For one more step

Not the whole road. Just the next stretch of it.

Don't carry your whole life in one thought, or every trouble that might come. Ask what in this moment is truly too much to bear.

Marcus Aurelius, Meditations

Most of the weight is in days that haven't happened yet. This moment, on its own, is usually lighter.

In the mountains of truth you never climb in vain: either you get higher today, or you train your strength to climb higher tomorrow.

Friedrich Nietzsche, Assorted Opinions and Maxims

Even the days that feel like no progress are building something.

Only God knows the worth of the smallest step taken on the road to good.

Concepción Arenal, Articles on Charity and Prisons

For someone you love who is going through it

Sometimes the strength is for someone else. These are short enough to send in a message, and none of them tells anyone how to feel.

Nothing gives us strength for the journey like words shared in love and company.

Miguel de Unamuno, My Religion and Other Short Essays

With you in the least, with you in the most, and always with you!

Amado Nervo, The Lotus Pond

Amado Nervo was a Mexican poet, and this line reads like a promise. It works well sent on its own.

Don't be afraid, for I am with you. Don't be dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you. Yes, I will help you.

Isaiah 41:10, Holy Bible

For someone whose strength comes from faith, this is a verse many people hold on to.

If it's more than a hard week

Quotes can keep you company. They aren't a treatment, and nobody should have to get through a crisis with a list of them. If things have felt dark for weeks, or you're struggling to function, please talk to a doctor, a therapist or someone you trust.

If you're thinking about ending your life or hurting yourself, reach out right now. In the US, call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. Elsewhere, call your local emergency number.

For the ordinary hard weeks, Dailies has topics that suit them, including You've done harder things, Be kinder to yourself and Rest without earning it. It keeps the lines coming quietly in the background, without asking anything of you.

Questions

What is a good quote about strength to keep going?

Rainer Maria Rilke's "Let everything happen to you: beauty and terror. Just keep going. No feeling is final" is one many people keep. For something shorter, Santiago Ramón y Cajal's "I didn't lose heart, in spite of everything" says a lot in a few words.

Where does "No feeling is final" come from?

From The Book of Hours, a collection of poems by Rainer Maria Rilke first published in 1905. The full line reads: "Let everything happen to you: beauty and terror. Just keep going. No feeling is final."

What can I send to someone who needs strength?

Something short that keeps them company. Amado Nervo's "With you in the least, with you in the most, and always with you!" works on its own as a message. If you want to add anything, a plain "thinking of you" is enough.

Does resting mean giving up?

Not to the people quoted here. Cervantes wrote in Don Quixote: "A bow cannot stay drawn forever, and frail human nature cannot keep going without some honest recreation." Rest is part of how people keep going.
