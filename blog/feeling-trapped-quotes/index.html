Feeling stuck in life quotes, for when you feel trapped

Trapped is a different feeling from stuck. Stuck is not knowing where to start. Trapped is knowing exactly where you are and not seeing the way out: the same job, the same routine, the same Tuesday. These are lines for that feeling. If yours is more about getting going at all, quotes for when you feel stuck is the place to start.

When you've outgrown the job or the routine

It fit once. That's the strange part. Nothing about the job or the routine went wrong. You kept growing, and it didn't.

Being tied by necessity to a single routine is existing, not living.

Montaigne, Essays

Today's you is not yesterday's or tomorrow's; as you change, let the ideal you shape of yourself change too.

Miguel de Unamuno, Essays

Unamuno's point is easy to miss: the picture you made of your life at twenty-five was a draft. You're allowed to redraw it.

We honor old foundations, but must never give up the right to start building again from scratch somewhere.

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe, Maxims and Reflections

When you're stuck in the same loop

Same alarm, same commute, same evening. Montaigne noticed something useful about this more than four centuries ago: the road doesn't change, but the way it feels does.

The same horse's gait feels rough one day and easy the next, and the same road shorter now, longer another time.

Montaigne, Essays

Today has one advantage over yesterday, which is gone: it exists, and it is made for us.

Maurice Maeterlinck, Wisdom and Destiny

Seize the unrepeatable newness of every moment, and don't stage-manage your joys.

André Gide, Fruits of the Earth

Gide's line is a small dare. Even a day that looks like yesterday has something new in it. Finding it doesn't break the loop, but it loosens it.

When you can't leave yet

Sometimes the door is right there and you still can't walk through it this month. The rent, the paperwork, the people who count on you. These are for the waiting part.

We know of caterpillars that build themselves a prison for one reason only: to grow wings.

Amado Nervo, Plenitude

From the outside, a cocoon looks a lot like a cell. Nervo, the Mexican poet, saw it the other way round: the walls are where the wings get made.

Pushing the hand of the clock will not make the hour come any sooner.

Victor Hugo, Postscript to My Life

Whoever has nothing still has a way out, as long as they still have hope.

Friedrich von Logau, Epigrams

When the way out is smaller than it looks

Feeling trapped makes the exit look enormous: quit everything, move away, start from zero. Usually the first door is much closer, and much smaller, than that.

My word, you're in a hurry! You're looking for the door and running right past it.

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe, Poems

The key hangs behind the door for all who know where to find it.

Amelia Earhart, 20 Hrs., 40 Min.

Earhart wrote that in her first book, 20 Hrs. 40 Min. The key isn't lost. It's just somewhere you haven't looked yet.

Some people would be free if only they could realize they are.

Marie von Ebner-Eschenbach, Aphorisms

Short quotes about being stuck

For a Lock Screen, a sticky note or a caption, when you want the whole thing in one breath.

Life is too short to stand still.

Alfred de Vigny, Servitude and Grandeur of Arms

I bend, and do not break.

Jean de La Fontaine, Fables

That's the reed talking, in La Fontaine's fable about an oak and a reed. The oak won't bend, and the storm pulls it up. The reed bends, and it's still there.

The wind is rising! We must try to live!

Paul Valéry, The Graveyard by the Sea

Valéry's line opens Hayao Miyazaki's film The Wind Rises. It's a good one to keep for the day something finally starts to move.

Where to go from here

If, underneath it all, the hard part is knowing where to start, quotes for when you feel stuck is the companion to this page. It's sorted by waiting to feel ready, pushing on the wrong door, and not being able to decide.

  • Pick the one that names your kind of trapped. The routine, the loop, the waiting.
  • Keep it where you'll see it on the day that keeps repeating. Your phone's Lock Screen, the mirror, the first page of a notebook.
  • Look for the small door. One thing you could do differently this week, not the whole escape.

Questions

What is a good quote for feeling trapped?

One that names it well is Montaigne's: "Being tied by necessity to a single routine is existing, not living." If you're somewhere you can't leave yet, Amado Nervo's line about caterpillars that build themselves a prison to grow wings is a kinder one to keep.

What are some short quotes about being stuck?

Three that fit on a Lock Screen: "Life is too short to stand still." (Alfred de Vigny), "I bend, and do not break." (Jean de La Fontaine) and "The wind is rising! We must try to live!" (Paul Valéry).

Who said "We know of caterpillars that build themselves a prison for one reason only: to grow wings"?

Amado Nervo, the Mexican poet, in Plenitude (Plenitud in Spanish), a book of short reflections in prose.

How is feeling trapped different from feeling stuck?

Stuck usually means not knowing where to start. Trapped usually means knowing exactly where you are and not seeing a way out, often in a routine, a job or a situation you can't leave yet. Different lines help with each, which is why this page sits beside quotes for when you feel stuck.
