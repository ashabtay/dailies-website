Quotes about ego: lines on quiet confidence

Pride, vanity and humility, in the words of people who thought about them for a long time. Grouped by when you'll want them: before you speak, after you've done something good, and when you notice yourself wanting the applause.

By Amit Shabtay, who makes Dailies Published 12 November 2026

Confidence that doesn't need an audience

The old writers didn't use the word ego the way we do. They wrote about pride, vanity and humility, and almost all of them arrived at the same place: the steadiest confidence is the quiet kind.

Humility was never about pretending to be less than you are. It sits halfway between thinking too little of yourself and too much.

Be modest without diffidence, proud without presumption.

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

Therefore, the Master knows herself but is not arrogant. She loves herself but also loves others. This is how she is able to make appropriate choices.

Lao Tzu, Tao Te Ching

A truly noble person keeps all their pride inside.

Ernest Renan, The Future of Science

It is as fine to be proud of yourself in private as it is ridiculous to be proud in front of others.

La Rochefoucauld, Maxims

In returning and rest you will be saved. In quietness and in confidence will be your strength.

Isaiah 30:15, Holy Bible

Let the work speak

The ego wants the credit. The work only wants doing. These lines put the attention back where it does some good.

If you are any good, stay quiet; it shows in its own time. Pose however you like, in the end it is the person that counts.

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe, Tame Xenia

Excellence should be in you; the praise, in others.

Baltasar Gracián, The Hero

The truly decent person is the one who doesn't show off about anything.

La Rochefoucauld, Maxims

When you have accomplished your goal simply walk away.

Lao Tzu, Tao Te Ching

Because she does not like to compete, no one can compete with the things she accomplishes.

Lao Tzu, Tao Te Ching

There is a noble pride in succeeding with very little: the pride of elegance and restraint.

Santiago Ramón y Cajal, Advice for a Young Investigator

Goodness is revered all the more for being simple, modest and free of all show.

François Fénelon, The Adventures of Telemachus

Humility as a strength

Humility is the solid foundation of all the virtues.

Confucius

Humility is invulnerability.

Marie von Ebner-Eschenbach, Aphorisms

Four words, and the whole idea. There's nothing to knock down in someone who isn't standing on a pedestal.

Humility and gentleness are not the opposite of strength; they fit it so well that strength cannot work without them.

Diego de Saavedra Fajardo, The Idea of a Christian Political Prince

There are three jewels that I cherish: compassion, moderation, and humility.

Lao Tzu, Tao Te Ching

The soft and pliable overcomes the hard and inflexible.

Lao Tzu, Tao Te Ching

Water is Lao Tzu's favorite picture of this. It takes the lowest place, gives way to everything, and wears down stone.

Humility, however deep, never unsettles, disturbs or upsets the soul; it comes with peace.

Saint Teresa of Ávila, The Way of Perfection

Nothing is more simple than greatness; indeed, to be simple is to be great.

Ralph Waldo Emerson

Free of what people think

Much of what we call ego is really a need for applause. These are for the moments you notice yourself waiting for it.

If your own approval is not enough for you, the world's applause is a poor bargain.

Johann Gottfried Seume, Apocrypha

As the wind cannot stir a solid mass of rock, so too blame and praise do not affect the wise.

Buddha, Dhammapada

Walk through praise and gossip alike, without being flattered by the one or beaten by the other.

Diego de Saavedra Fajardo, The Idea of a Christian Political Prince

She does not dwell on her past accomplishments, and does not glory in any praise.

Lao Tzu, Tao Te Ching

Some work has no reward but self-respect and the approval of those who know.

Santiago Ramón y Cajal, Recollections of My Life

Proud of being humble

The humblebrag isn't new. Henry Ward Beecher spotted it in the 1800s, and the philosopher Henri Bergson saw what sits underneath it.

Some people are proud of their humility.

Henry Ward Beecher

We care about praise and honors exactly as much as we are unsure we have succeeded. There is modesty at the bottom of vanity.

Henri Bergson, Mind-Energy

Questions

What is a good quote about ego?

Goethe's "Be modest without diffidence, proud without presumption" says it in one line. For humility as a strength, Marie von Ebner-Eschenbach's "Humility is invulnerability" is hard to beat.

Who said "Humility is the solid foundation of all the virtues"?

Confucius, the Chinese teacher and philosopher. The line is credited to him in the classic quotation dictionaries.

What is the difference between ego and confidence?

Confidence is trusting what you can do. Ego is needing other people to see it. The writers here keep the first and let go of the second: do the work well, and leave the applause to take care of itself.

What did the Buddha say about praise and blame?

In the Dhammapada: "As the wind cannot stir a solid mass of rock, so too blame and praise do not affect the wise." The wise stay steady whichever way the wind blows.
