Monday motivation: how to start the week well

Monday gets a bad name, but a new week is one of the easiest places to start something. Here's what the research says about making Monday work for you, and some lines worth reading before the week begins.

By Amit Shabtay, who makes Dailies Published 13 November 2026

Why Monday is a good day to start

Monday has a bad name, but it has one real advantage: it feels like a clean page. In 2014 Hengchen Dai, Katherine Milkman and Jason Riis published a paper in Management Science on what they called the fresh start effect. Google searches for "diet" rose at the start of each week, and visits to a university gym rose at the start of each week, month and semester. A new week gives the mind permission to leave last week behind.

So the urge to start something on a Monday isn't silly. It's a small push that comes round fifty-two times a year. The trick is to make good use of it.

Start Monday on Friday

Before you stop on Friday, write down the first thing you'll do on Monday, and where you'll do it. E. J. Masicampo and Roy Baumeister found, in studies published in the Journal of Personality and Social Psychology in 2011, that making a concrete plan for an unfinished task quiets the thoughts that keep circling back to it. A written plan lets the weekend be a weekend, and on Monday morning the decision is already made.

What matters is the beginning. The rest takes care of itself.

Ernst von Feuchtersleben, The Dietetics of the Soul

Better to start gently and coolly, and save your breath and big pushes for the thick and the finish of the work.

Montaigne, Essays

Make the first task a small one

Teresa Amabile and Steven Kramer read thousands of daily diary entries from people at work, and published what they found in The Progress Principle in 2011. The days people felt best about were the days they made progress on something that mattered to them, even a small step. So put a task you can finish first on Monday's list. A small win at nine o'clock sets the tone for the rest of the week.

Every beginning is small.

Joseph Joubert, Thoughts, Essays and Maxims

Just start. Start slow if you have to. Start small if you have to. Start privately if you have to. Just start.

James Clear, 3-2-1 Newsletter

You don't start a pyramid at the top.

Romain Rolland, Jean-Christophe

Give Monday something to look forward to

Katherine Milkman, Julia Minson and Kevin Volpp tested an idea they called temptation bundling: people could listen to an audiobook they loved, but only at the gym. They went to the gym more. The same trick works on a whole day. Save something you enjoy for Monday: the good coffee, a call with a friend, the podcast you've been saving, lunch outside. It gives the day a reason of its own.

The best way to start each day well: on waking, think whether you could give at least one person some joy today.

Friedrich Nietzsche, Human, All Too Human

Be reverent before the rising day. Don't think about what will be in a year, in ten years. Think about today.

Romain Rolland, Jean-Christophe

Monday motivation quotes

A few more for the start of the week. Any of them works as a Monday post, a note on the fridge, or the first thing you read before work.

Every beginning is bright; the threshold is the place of expectation.

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe, Wilhelm Meister's Apprenticeship

You can begin wherever you choose.

Alphonse Gratry, The Well-Springs

I see only one way to find out how far you can go: set out and start walking.

Henri Bergson, Mind-Energy

I'm glad you begin again every day: there's no better way to make progress than always beginning again.

Saint Francis de Sales, Letters

Rest and renewal, a fresh start and hope: a day has ended, long live tomorrow!

Henri-Frédéric Amiel, Amiel's Journal

Questions

How do I get motivated on a Monday?

Decide Monday's first task before you stop on Friday, make it something small you can finish, and save something you enjoy for Monday. A small early win and a plan already made do more than waiting to feel motivated.

Why do people start new habits on Mondays?

Researchers call it the fresh start effect. Hengchen Dai, Katherine Milkman and Jason Riis found that searches for "diet" and gym visits rise at the start of a week, month or year. A new week feels like a clean page.

What is a good Monday motivation quote?

George Sand's "Everything has one beautiful moment: the beginning" suits a Monday well. For getting going, Joseph Joubert's "Every beginning is small" is short enough to remember all week.

What is Monday motivation?

It's the habit of sharing something encouraging at the start of the week, often under the #MondayMotivation hashtag. The best kind is short and gives you a reason to begin, not a lecture.
