Christmas quotes for cards, messages and the season

Most Christmas cards are trying to say the same few warm things: I'm glad you're in my life, I hope your home is full, the light will come back. The quotes below are lines for the season rather than lines about Christmas itself. They're about giving, light, home and kindness, and they're sorted by where you might want to use them.

For a Christmas card

A card has room for one good line and a few words of your own. These two suit almost anyone on the list.

Is our happiness not bound together for good, and is the joy of one not the joy of the other?

Ernest Renan, Letters from the Seminary

This one is for the person whose good news this year felt like your own.

Quotes about giving

The presents people remember are rarely the expensive ones. They're the ones that show someone was paying attention.

A kind heart makes the giving good.

Lao Tzu, Tao Te Ching

Generosity is not in giving me that which I need more than you do, but it is in giving me that which you need more than I do.

Kahlil Gibran, Sand and Foam

Gibran's line belongs in a card for someone who gave more than they could spare this year.

We love far more strongly when we are planning to give someone a joy than an hour after we have given it.

Jean Paul Richter, Flower, Fruit and Thorn Pieces

Anyone who has hidden a present at the back of a closet for weeks knows this one is true.

Light in the dark of the year

In the northern hemisphere, Christmas comes a few days after the longest night of the year, and so many of its traditions are about light: candles, lights in the windows, a lit tree in a dark room.

The people who walked in darkness have seen a great light.

Isaiah 9:2, Holy Bible

One of the traditional readings for Christmas, and the one line of scripture here, for readers who keep the season with faith.

It is at night that it's beautiful to believe in the light!

Edmond Rostand, Chantecler

Rostand gave this line to Chantecler, a rooster who believes his crowing makes the sun come up. Not a bad thing to believe in December.

Sunlight is not enough. We need the light of the heart.

Romain Rolland, The Life of Tolstoy

Home and the people around the table

For everyone around the table, and everyone who traveled to get there.

No light is lovelier than the cloud of smoke rising to the sky from a home's hearth.

Ramón de Campoamor, Witticisms

Campoamor wrote it as a two-line poem. A chimney smoking on a cold evening means someone is home and the fire is lit.

Home is not where you live, but where you are understood.

Christian Morgenstern, Stages

Many a one has been comforted in their sorrow by seeing a good dish come upon the table.

Elizabeth Gaskell, Cranford

Gaskell's line is for whoever does the cooking, and for any year when someone is missing from the table.

Kindness and good will

Peace and good will are about as old as Christmas wishes get. These two keep it simple.

Kindness in how we treat each other is the chief charm of life.

Henri Lacordaire, Letters to Young Men

Love and passion can fly away, but goodwill will win forever.

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe, Tame Xenia

Short Christmas quotes for a message

For a text, a gift tag or a caption, where there's room for a few words and no more.

You can only have peace if you give it.

Marie von Ebner-Eschenbach, Aphorisms

Two together can do more than one alone.

Saint John of the Cross, Spiritual Counsels and Maxims

To have love is not to lose the light.

Victor Hugo, Les Misérables

Using them in a card or a message

  • Put the quote first and your own line after it. A good quote opens the card. A sentence about this person and this year is what they'll keep.
  • Match the line to the person. A giving line for the most generous person you know, a home line for family, a short one for a gift tag.
  • Keep the author's name. It's only fair, and it lets the reader go and find more.
  • Short ones travel best. On a phone screen or under a photo, a line of ten words or fewer reads at a glance.

Questions

What is a good quote for a Christmas card?

Joseph Joubert's "Warmth and a good welcome are an invitation that stays open all year." suits almost anyone. For family, Christian Morgenstern's "Home is not where you live, but where you are understood." is a lovely choice.

What is a short Christmas quote for a message?

Marie von Ebner-Eschenbach's "You can only have peace if you give it." and Victor Hugo's "To have love is not to lose the light." both fit in a text or on a gift tag.

What should I write in a Christmas card besides a quote?

One line that only fits this person: something you shared this year, something you're grateful for, or something you'd like to do together next year. A specific sentence means more than a clever one.

Is there a Bible verse about light for Christmas?

One of the traditional readings for Christmas is Isaiah 9:2: "The people who walked in darkness have seen a great light."
