Motivational quotes for work, for you and your team

Most quotes about work sound like a poster in an office lobby. These are older and plainer. They're sorted by when you'd reach for one: the start of a week, a piece of work you care about, a message to your team, a day that went badly. Keep one for yourself, or pass it on.

To start the workday or week

The first hour is when the work looks biggest. These are for that hour, on a Monday or any other day.

On mornings you don't want to get up, keep this thought close: I'm rising to do the work of a human being.

Marcus Aurelius, Meditations

An emperor, writing to himself. Even he had mornings like that.

Don't tell them how far the whole journey is, only how far to go today.

Saint Francis de Sales, Letters

Believe, hope and smile, and with all that, get to work.

Alain, Alain on Happiness

If Monday is the hard part, Monday motivation has more for the start of the week.

For doing good work

Not the hustle kind. The kind where you care how the thing turns out, whatever the thing is.

Neither the harp nor the broom makes the difference. Everything depends on the hand that holds them, and the spirit that moves that hand.

Charles Wagner, The Simple Life

A harp and a broom, side by side. Whatever your job looks like from the outside, this one is about the hand.

Working every day to improve the humblest task is already a masterpiece in itself.

Amado Nervo, On the War

Do things well for your own sake, not for the noise they make.

Alfred de Vigny, Servitude and Grandeur of Arms

Genius begins beautiful works, but only work finishes them.

Joseph Joubert, Thoughts, Essays and Maxims

For anyone holding a draft that's nearly done.

For the team

Lines to share with coworkers, drop into the team chat or read out to open a meeting. The short ones work best said out loud.

Hard and free, together: not easy work, and not forced work. Ask people for their best memory of a job, and it's often a stretch like that.

The more different voices you can bring together, the more wonderful the song tends to sound.

Angelus Silesius, The Cherubinic Wanderer

A seventeenth-century poet on singing together, and a good line to open a meeting of people who don't think alike.

Need a lift? Think of the good in the people around you: how hard one works, how humble another is, how generous a third.

Marcus Aurelius, Meditations

Worth reading slowly: one person, then another, then a third. It's hard to stay annoyed at a team while you do it.

For a hard day at work

The meeting went badly, the inbox won, or the day was simply long. A few lines for the end of it.

No work is without hope.

Joseph Roux, Meditations of a Parish Priest

I even believe we do nothing as new and fresh as on certain tired days.

Charles Péguy, Victor-Marie, Count Hugo

A strange comfort, and a real one: a tired day isn't a wasted day.

Your mind can turn whatever blocks your work into help. The obstacle in the road carries you along the road.

Marcus Aurelius, Meditations

For ambition without burning out

Wanting big things from your work is good. These lines keep the big things and lose the rush.

Set your aim and your hope further off, past the lure of quick success; lasting worth is harder to reach, but worthier.

José Ingenieros, The Mediocre Man

All wisdom is slow; all creating takes the long way round.

Christian Morgenstern, Stages

Work and rest go together, hand in hand.

Rabindranath Tagore, Glimpses of Bengal

Rest isn't the opposite of the work here. It walks beside it.

Keeping one close

A quote for work does its job at the right moment: before the first email, or at four in the afternoon when the day sags. Save the one that fits, or send it to someone on your team who could use it today.

Questions

What is a good motivational quote for work?

For a team, Alain's "The greatest human pleasure is probably hard, free work done together" is hard to beat. For yourself on a slow morning, Marcus Aurelius has the line: "I'm rising to do the work of a human being."

What is a good quote to share with coworkers or a team?

Short lines read best in a chat or at the start of a meeting. Angelus Silesius's "The more different voices you can bring together, the more wonderful the song tends to sound" suits a mixed team, and Marcus Aurelius's "Think of the good in the people around you" is a kind one to pass on.

What is a good quote for a hard day at work?

Joseph Roux said it in five words: "No work is without hope." Charles Péguy goes further: "I even believe we do nothing as new and fresh as on certain tired days."

Who said "The obstacle in the road carries you along the road"?

Marcus Aurelius, in the Meditations, the private notebook he kept while he ruled Rome. It's the idea behind the saying "the obstacle is the way." There's more of him in Marcus Aurelius quotes in plain English.
