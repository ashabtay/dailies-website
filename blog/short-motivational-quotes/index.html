Short motivational quotes for every day

Some days you don't need a paragraph. You need a few words you can carry around. These are short enough for a caption, a bio, a sticky note or your Lock Screen, and they still say something.

To start something

For the first step, when the whole thing still looks too big.

Every day has a different flower.

Stendhal, On Love

Have faith in small efforts.

Alain, Alain on Happiness

Every seed is a longing.

Kahlil Gibran, Sand and Foam

Learning without doing is wasted.

Derek Sivers, sive.rs

The world is bigger than you think.

Ernest Renan, Recollections of My Youth

To keep going

For the middle part, when the start is behind you and the finish is nowhere in sight.

Patience, and shuffle the cards.

Miguel de Cervantes, Don Quixote

From Don Quixote: a whole attitude to a bad run of luck in a handful of words. Wait, then shuffle again.

Habit is the great motive-power.

Fyodor Dostoevsky, The Brothers Karamazov

Do not turn one mistake into two.

Baltasar Gracián, The Art of Worldly Wisdom

Patience is the art of hoping.

There is always hope!

Some flaws are not flaws at all.

For courage

For the message you haven't sent and the thing you haven't tried yet.

Dare to be wrong.

Leo Babauta, Zen Habits

You have to dare to put some color on your wings!

Edmond Rostand, Chantecler

From Chantecler, Rostand's play about a rooster who believes his song makes the sun rise. A good line for the day you post something bolder than usual.

Montaigne's own motto. It still makes a good bio for anyone who is curious about everything.

Joseph Joubert, Thoughts, Essays and Maxims

Where a short quote works best

A short line earns its place wherever there is no room for a long one:

  • A caption. Under a photo, a few words say what the picture can't. Add the author's name; it's a few more words and it's only fair.
  • A bio. One line can say more about you than a list of jobs. Pick one that will still sound like you next year.
  • A note. On a sticky note, the bathroom mirror, a lunchbox. The shorter it is, the more likely it gets read.
  • Your Lock Screen. You see it every time you pick up your phone, which makes it the best spot for a line you want to remember.

Questions

What is a good short motivational quote?

Alain's "Have faith in small efforts." is hard to beat for a start, and Cervantes's "Patience, and shuffle the cards." suits a bad day. For something calmer, try Victor Hugo's "The bottom of the sea is always calm."

What is a good short quote for a bio?

One that sounds like you. Cervantes's "I know who I am." is confident without showing off, Montaigne's "What do I know?" is curious, and Leo Babauta's "Dare to be wrong." suits anyone who makes things.

How short should a Lock Screen quote be?

Short enough to take in at a glance, so a line or two. You read your Lock Screen on the way to something else, and a long quote tends to get skipped.

Where can I see a short quote every day?

Somewhere you already look, like your Lock Screen or Home Screen. Dailies puts a quote there that changes through the day, from topics you pick, so a good line finds you without you going looking for it.
