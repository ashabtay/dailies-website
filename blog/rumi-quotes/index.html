Who Rumi was

Jalal al-Din Rumi was a Persian poet and Sufi teacher. He was born in 1207, traditionally in Balkh, in what is now Afghanistan, and spent most of his life in Konya, in what is now Turkey, where he died in 1273.

His two great works are the Masnavi, a long poem of stories and teaching in six books, and the Divan of Shams of Tabriz, a collection of lyric poems named for the wandering mystic who became his closest friend.

Where these lines come from

Each line is rendered into plain, current English from public-domain translations: E. H. Whinfield's abridged Masnavi (1898), and R. A. Nicholson's Selected Poems from the Divani Shamsi Tabriz (1898) and his Mathnawi (1926 and 1930). Many lines shared online as Rumi's are loose modern versions, or not his at all. These stay close to what the old translators found on the page.

Questions

What is a good Rumi quote to start with?

“Every autumn holds a spring hidden inside it.” is one people keep. For love, try “We are pieces of steel, and your love is the magnet.”

Are these real Rumi quotes?

Yes. Every line here comes from the Masnavi or the Divan of Shams of Tabriz, by way of the public-domain translations named above, and the book is named under each one. Many popular “Rumi quotes” come from loose modern versions, or from no poem of his at all.

Can I put a Rumi quote on my phone?

Yes. Under each line, Make it a wallpaper puts it on a Lock Screen picture for free, and Send it in an envelope sends it to someone. Dailies, the app this site is for, puts a new line on your Lock Screen every day.
